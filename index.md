## Featured free demos

### [DataStudio Lite](https://github.com/tinkomi/datastudio-offline-converter)
JSON, CSV, and Markdown conversion with table preview and local downloads. The current interface is Korean-language.

### [SnapMock Studio Lite](https://github.com/tinkomi/snapmock-studio-lite)
A browser-based screenshot presentation and isometric mockup tool with PNG export.

### [SparSpan Lite](https://github.com/tinkomi/sparspan-rigging-path-takeoff-demo)
A view-only v1.0.0 miniature rigging takeoff demonstration with 10 fixed points and 7 paths.

### [RegisterBench Lite](https://github.com/tinkomi/registerbench-lite-read-only-proof-review-demo)
A read-only review sample with three fictional proofs and vector sketches.

### [Community Photo Archive Lite](https://github.com/tinkomi/community-photo-archive-digitization-lite)
An editable Excel starter with 10 record rows and three fictional examples.
